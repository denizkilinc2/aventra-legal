Delete Your Account

You can delete your Aventra account yourself, at any time, directly inside the app. No request or approval is needed.

Deleting from inside the app

  1. Open the Aventra app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, to the Danger zone section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Enter your password and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

If you can no longer access the app

Email support@useaventra.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject line Delete my account. We verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (name, email, username, password)Deleted immediately
Trip plans and stop progressDeleted immediately
Journal entries and uploaded photosDeleted immediately
Favourites and packing listsDeleted immediately
Active plan or entitlementDeleted immediately
Analytics eventsDeleted automatically after 90 days
Hash of your phone numberRetained — see below

One exception

If you claimed the free trial, a one-way cryptographic hash of your phone number is retained to prevent the trial from being claimed again. The original number cannot be recovered from this hash, it is never shared, and it is used for no other purpose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Deleting your Aventra account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it in your Google Play account settings.
