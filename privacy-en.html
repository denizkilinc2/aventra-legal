Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026
Türkçe sürüm
In short: Aventra collects the minimum data needed to run the app. We don't sell your data and we don't share it with ad networks. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Aventra is an AI-assisted travel planning app. In this policy, "we" refers to the party that develops and operates the app. Contact: support@useaventra.com

If you start a free trial

We store your phone number as a cryptographic hash. The raw number is never kept in our database. Its only purpose is to make sure the free trial is used once per person. We never use your number for marketing and never share it.

Technical data collected automatically

To understand which screens are used and where the app fails, we keep limited event records: event name, session ID, platform (iOS/Android), app version and timestamp. These records are deleted automatically after 90 days. We do not collect advertising identifiers, we do not track you across apps, and we use no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Share links

When you share a trip plan or a journal entry, a public link to that content is created. Anyone with the link can view that content. You can turn sharing off from inside the app at any time, and the link stops working. The shared page shows only that content — your profile, email and other records are not visible.

5. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Data is stored on servers within the European Union.
  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Analytics events are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Unused verification codes are deleted after 7 days.
  • When you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately and permanently.

6. Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Danger zone → Delete account. Once you enter your password and confirm, your account, journal entries, trips, favourites, all uploaded photos and any active plan are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone, and you don't need to request it from us.

One exception: the hash of your phone number is retained to prevent the free trial from being claimed again. The original number cannot be recovered from this hash, and it is used for no other purpose.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR in the EU, KVKK in Türkiye), you have the right to:

  • Know what data of yours is processed
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data deleted (you can do this yourself in the app)
  • Object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, write to support@useaventra.com. We respond to requests within 30 days at the latest.

8. Children's privacy

Aventra is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we become aware that such data has been collected, we delete it. If you believe your child has sent us data, please get in touch.

9. Security

  • All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text.
  • Phone numbers are stored only as cryptographic hashes.
  • We never see payment details — payments are processed by Apple and Google.

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable and current measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date on this page. For significant changes, we will notify you inside the app.

11. Contact

For any privacy question: support@useaventra.com
